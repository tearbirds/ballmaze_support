Privacy Policy

Ball In A Maze / Last updated: 23 September 2026

日本語版

Katsumi Sakurai ("we") sets out below how information is handled in the iOS app "Ball In A Maze" ("the app").

1. Information we do not collect

The app has no account or sign-in, and we operate no server. We never receive any information about you.

The app does not use or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photo library or microphone. Camera use is described in section 2.

2. Camera

Eight of the forty stages (21-24 and 37-40) have a glass floor. On those stages the app can display the rear camera feed underneath the translucent board, so that whatever is behind the device appears to show through it.

What the app does
DisplayFrames are rendered on screen, and nothing else
Recording / savingNever. The app does not access the photo library at all
AnalysisNever. There is no face or object recognition of any kind
TransmissionNever. No frame leaves the device. Nothing is passed to advertising
When it runsOnly while you are on a glass-floor stage with the "see-through" look selected. It stops as soon as you change the look or leave the stage

The look can be switched at any time between see-through, frosted and opaque, using the "ガラス" button at the bottom of the screen or the Settings screen. The camera is not used for frosted or opaque.

If you decline camera permission, every stage remains fully playable. You can change the permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

3. What is stored on your device

Only the following is stored on the device itself (iOS UserDefaults). None of it is transmitted.

TypeContents
ProgressHow far you have cleared
Best timesYour fastest time for each stage
StarsStars collected per stage (up to three)
SettingsControl style, sound, haptics, glass floor look

This data is removed from the device when the app is deleted. We cannot view or obtain it.

4. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display one banner advertisement at the bottom of the screen. AdMob may automatically collect the following for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

TypeContents
Advertising identifiersIDFA and identifiers issued by AdMob
Device informationDevice model, OS version, language, screen size
Approximate regionCountry or region inferred from the IP address
Ad interactionsImpressions and taps

This information is collected and used by Google; we do not receive its contents. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Camera frames are never used for advertising. No frame is included in anything sent to AdMob.

5. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On first launch iOS asks whether the app may track you.

Your choiceWhat happens
AllowAds may be personalised using the IDFA
Ask app not to trackThe IDFA is not used. Ads are still shown, but are not personalised

Either way, every stage remains fully playable. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

6. For users in the EU and UK (consent)

Depending on your region, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is presented before ads are shown. Your choices can be changed at any time from "広告のプライバシー設定" (Ad privacy settings) on the app's Settings screen.

7. In-app purchases

The app has no in-app purchases.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the law or the app changes. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
