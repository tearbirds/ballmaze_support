Privacy Policy

むかしあそび / Mukashi Asobi (formerly Ball In A Maze) / Last updated: 16 November 2026

日本語版

Katsumi Sakurai ("we") sets out below how information is handled in the iOS app "むかしあそび" / Mukashi Asobi, formerly "Ball In A Maze" ("the app"). The app contains three games - goldfish scooping (きんぎょすくい), the tilt maze (玉ころがし) and picture matching (えあわせ) - and this policy applies to all of them.

1. Information we do not collect

The app has no account or sign-in, and we operate no server. We never receive any information about you.

The app does not use or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or microphone. Photos you choose for えあわせ stay on your device and never reach us. Camera use is described in section 2, photos in section 3.

2. Camera

Eight of the forty stages (21-24 and 37-40) have a glass floor. On those stages the app can display the rear camera feed underneath the translucent board, so that whatever is behind the device appears to show through it.

What the app does
DisplayFrames are rendered on screen, and nothing else
Recording / savingNever. Camera frames are not written to the photo library
AnalysisNever. There is no face or object recognition of any kind
TransmissionNever. No frame leaves the device. Nothing is passed to advertising
When it runsOnly while you are on a glass-floor stage with the "see-through" look selected. It stops as soon as you change the look or leave the stage

The look can be switched at any time between see-through, frosted and opaque, using the "ガラス" button at the bottom of the screen or the Settings screen. The camera is not used for frosted or opaque.

If you decline camera permission, every stage remains fully playable. You can change the permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

3. Photos

えあわせ (picture matching) is played with photographs, which are cut into four pieces and put back together. You choose where the photos come from:

SourcePermissionWhat happens
Photos bundled with the appNone144 photos ship with the app. This is the default
Pick your own photosNoneThe iOS photo picker (PhotosPicker). The app does not read your library; it receives only the photos you picked (up to 100)
Use a whole albumRequiredListing albums needs access to the photo library. Up to 100 photos are then imported from the album you choose

Photos you choose are cropped to a square and stored inside the app's own folder on your device so that the board can draw them.

What the app does
TransmissionNone. Photos never leave your device, and they are never passed to advertising
Access by usImpossible. We operate no server and never receive your photos
AnalysisFace positions are detected on the device (iOS Vision) only to choose where to crop, so that faces are not cut in half. The result is used for the crop and is neither stored nor sent
DeletionImported photos are removed when you choose another source, or when you delete the app

You can play えあわせ without granting photo library access: the bundled photos and the picker (which needs no permission) both remain available. You can change the permission at any time in iOS Settings - Privacy & Security - Photos - むかしあそび.

4. What is stored on your device

Only the following is stored on the device itself (iOS UserDefaults). None of it is transmitted.

TypeContents
ProgressHow far you have cleared
Best timesYour fastest time for each stage
StarsStars collected per stage (up to three)
Best scoresYour best score in goldfish scooping and the number of photos completed in picture matching
SettingsControl style, sound, haptics, glass floor look, photo source

This data is removed from the device when the app is deleted. We cannot view or obtain it.

5. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob. Three ad formats are shown.

FormatWhen it appears
BannerOne banner at the top of the screen while you play
InterstitialMay appear when a game of goldfish scooping or picture matching ends
Rewarded videoPlayed only if you choose "watch an ad for one more poi" in goldfish scooping. You can keep playing without watching it

AdMob may automatically collect the following for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

TypeContents
Advertising identifiersIDFA and identifiers issued by AdMob
Device informationDevice model, OS version, language, screen size
Approximate regionCountry or region inferred from the IP address
Ad interactionsImpressions and taps

This information is collected and used by Google; we do not receive its contents. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Camera frames are never used for advertising. No frame is included in anything sent to AdMob.

6. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On first launch iOS asks whether the app may track you.

Your choiceWhat happens
AllowAds may be personalised using the IDFA
Ask app not to trackThe IDFA is not used. Ads are still shown, but are not personalised

Either way, every stage remains fully playable. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

7. For users in the EU and UK (consent)

Depending on your region, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is presented before ads are shown. Your choices can be changed at any time from "広告のプライバシー設定" (Ad privacy settings) on the app's Settings screen.

8. In-app purchases

The app has no in-app purchases. The coins in goldfish scooping exist only inside the game; they cannot be bought with money or exchanged for anything.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the law or the app changes. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
